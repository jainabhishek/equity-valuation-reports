What's in each memo

Every company folder pairs a self-contained HTML memo with a live Excel model. Alphabet's workbook is a second implementation rather than a printout: a formula engine recalculates its dated FCFF periods, share bridge, WACC, terminal mechanics, scenario targets and decision outputs.

  • memo.html — decision, causal thesis, implementation gate, scenario skew, quarterly catalyst bridge, conditional action rules, sources and limitations
  • model.xlsx — live model: formula-linked D&A vintages, CAPM, shares, dated DCF, scenarios, sensitivity, decision and blocking checks.
  • build/ — reproducible model and rendering pipeline with independent workbook recalculation.
