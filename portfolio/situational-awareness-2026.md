## Thesis basis

Leopold Aschenbrenner's June 2024 essay decomposes into five investable pillars. Scorecard as of August 2026 (verified against primary sources — SEC filings, Nvidia earnings, METR, EIA):

| Pillar | Status | Crowding |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Compute scaling ($1T/yr investment by 2027) | On track/ahead ($725B hyperscaler capex guided 2026) | Extreme in mega-caps |
| 2. Power is the binding constraint | On track (multiple 1–2 GW clusters live) | Moderate; IPPs *down* YTD |
| 3. Agents replace cognitive labor by ~2027 | Roughly on track (METR 50% horizon ≥16h, doubling ~4.3mo) | Long side crowded |
| 4. US–China security race | Vindicated (distillation campaigns, espionage conviction) | Light |
| 5. Government project by 27/28 | Partially on track (Genesis Mission, Intel stake) | Barely priced |

**The July 2026 dislocation.** Markets repriced the capex-ahead-of-revenue gap in late July (the Situational Awareness LP margin-call liquidation was both symptom and accelerant, with ~$35B of forced selling absorbed by Citadel). Result: first-order AI names (NVDA −0.6% from high, AVGO −4%, COHR −3%) barely corrected, while the thesis's second-derivative names — memory, powered land, neoclouds, on-site power — sit 20–45% below their June highs *with the underlying buildout evidence unchanged* (capex guides intact, Nvidia Q1 FY27 DC revenue $75.2B +92% YoY, gigawatt campuses under construction). The maximum-return expression for the remainder of 2026 is therefore concentrated in crash-discounted phase-2 names, not the crowded phase-1 names.

## Catalysts, rest of 2026

1. **Nvidia Q2 FY27 earnings (late Aug)** — first post-crash read on whether the $725B capex wave is intact. The single biggest risk/catalyst for the whole sleeve.
2. **Micron FQ1 (Sep) + memory contract pricing** — HBM/NAND pricing momentum is the load-bearing wall under MU/SNDK.
3. **Hyperscaler Q3 prints (Oct)** — 2027 capex guidance; Evercore/BofA already project >$1T combined.
4. **Microsoft AI run rate update** — $37B at +123% YoY; the next print is the test of the thesis's weakest link (monetization).
5. **METR horizon updates** — next doubling due ~Q4 2026; continued ~4.3-month doubling keeps the agent narrative (and pillar 3) on its 2027 track.
6. **Genesis Mission expansion** — $5B+ committed July 2026; follow-on awards benefit INTC, nuclear names, national-lab-adjacent compute.
7. **Powered-land M&A** — hyperscalers shopping for interconnected capacity post-crash; CORZ/IREN/APLD are the shelf.

## Kill criteria (trim/exit triggers — decided in advance)

- **Two consecutive hyperscaler capex guide-downs** → thesis pillar 1 breaking; cut the sleeve, don't average down.
- **Microsoft AI run rate prints < $50B at the January 2027 update** (vs. $37B at +123% now) → monetization lag worsening, not compressing; halve high-beta weights.
- **Memory spot/contract pricing rolls over two straight months** → exit SNDK, halve MU.
- **METR doubling time stretches past ~7 months** → pillar-3 narrative slipping to 2028+; reduce neocloud/powered-land.
- **Any name −30% from entry without a thesis-relevant catalyst** → forced review, no exceptions.

## Risk notes

- **This is one factor, not twelve names.** In a repeat of late July, everything here falls together; expect 30–50% peak-to-trough sleeve drawdowns. The July episode was survivable precisely for unlevered holders — the design constraint of this portfolio.
- **Maximizing 2026 return means accepting that a Q4 sentiment turn can erase the year.** The kill criteria above are the only defense; they replace the leverage-margin-call feedback loop with pre-committed exits.
- **Concentration in the crash-discounted names is a bet that July was a positioning unwind, not an information event.** The evidence (capex guides intact, Nvidia acceleration, contract books unchanged) supports that reading today; the catalysts list is where it gets falsified.
- Forced-seller overhang: Citadel absorbed ~$35B of this exact book in July; residual distribution may cap near-term upside in SA LP's former positions (BE, SNDK, CORZ, IREN, CRWV overlap).
